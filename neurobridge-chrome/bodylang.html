🧍 Nonverbal cue explorer (optional, experimental)

Describes a few observable body cues in view of your camera, as a learning aid. It never tells you what someone thinks or feels.

Before you start
  • The camera turns on only when you press Start, and turns off when you press Stop or close this tab.
  • Video is analysed on this device. It is not recorded, saved, or uploaded.
  • If other people are in view, tell them first.
  • The same cue can mean different things for different people, cultures, and situations. One cue alone tells you very little.

Sit or stand facing the camera with your shoulders level for a few seconds. NeuroBridge uses that as your own baseline.
